## 7. Onboarding, graduation day, grace and appeals

**Three venues with three jobs.** On registration the current-cohort student joins their **skill training WhatsApp group**. Later they also join a **general onboarding WhatsApp group for all skills** for updates and cross-skill connections. The shared live event itself is on the **KR8 YouTube channel**, not one of those group chats. Staff update the link on the public website, in student profiles and in the onboarding group. Future cohorts use the welcome-group behaviour in §3.

In the week before the event, newcomers hear directly from outgoing learners. At the shared event, **graduation comes first**: announce graduate numbers and results, release signed certificates at the admin-scheduled time, then proceed to onboarding. Not an elaborate ceremony. Do not send a signed certificate early. Returning learners may miss orientation without losing their place; they know the process and can enquire about changes. Freshers missing it is a serious matter.

## 9. Scoring, growth, review and recovery

1. **Grade every assignment and the final project**, one learner at a time. Coach can assess on WhatsApp immediately, enter marks before or after sending the submission code, and give corrections later. Entry can happen later still and must map to the **original student, date and assignment**. A trusted delegate may **transcribe a coach-awarded grade**; it appears immediately in the student profile, with no second approval. Distinguish assessor from data-entry actor. An active student rep cannot grade classmates.
2. **Final grade = 0.70 × assignment average + 0.30 × final project. Pass = 80% across all seven skills.** Example: 80 assignment average and 90 project = **83**. The site calculates the result; no manual final-total entry. There is no accepted 90% threshold/80–89 grace band: that was an older repo policy. The final grade is only one gate: class ≥80%, assignment attendance 100%, Mindset Shift requirement, assessed work, and certification/release steps still apply.
3. **Acknowledged, assessed, corrected and improved are distinct.** An acknowledged unmarked piece is *Awaiting grade*, not zero. Correcting a transcription error is not a student revision. When a student acts on feedback and resubmits, the coach may award a **limited improvement bonus; the coach decides the points**, not a fixed +5/+10/+15 Academy cap. Never exceed 100 on an assignment. Retain original score, bonus and revised score; recognise the behaviour as **"Acted on feedback"** or equivalent. Do not count a second submission attendance. Exact public presentation of this recognition: **Open**.
4. **Review:** student can flag individual marks when they appear. Once **all** required assignments and the final project have marks, automatically calculate the provisional final grade, notify, and allow **72 hours from notification** to flag an error. Staff marking delays must not manufacture missing student work. A changed grade in final review → recalculate, re-notify, **fresh 72 hours**; student may accept the corrected grade early. Unresolved queries hold sign-off. Adult result stays private until credential release.
5. **Recovery:** one week after the cohort ends for make-up/revision. This is not the 72-hour clerical/grade review and does not automatically waive attendance. If still unsuccessful, repeat **the same skill** in a later cohort, fresh from week one: new attendance, assignments, Mindset Shift and grades. Keep earlier attempt history; do not transfer credit. Removed students need coach clearance. Different free skill stays locked absent an approved critical switch.

### Monthly honours and exceptional work

- A teacher can feature exceptional work. Give the verification record concrete credit for the work/skill, not just a decorative badge. This is not a KR8FOLIO portfolio. Do not silently treat WhatsApp submissions as permission for a public feature.
- **Best student in each skill each month:** grades make the shortlist **together with all attendance**: classes, assignment submissions and Mindset Shift. The coach chooses the winner from the shortlist; not simply highest mark. **Open:** shortlist formula/size.
- **Overall best student each month:** compare the coach-selected skill winners by a published score, not a second vote. **60% monthly grades + 15% class attendance + 15% assignment submission + 10% Mindset Shift attendance.** Use percentages so one extra class is not an automatic advantage. Record month, cohort, skill and honour. Do not substitute this formula for graduation's 70/30 grade. **Open:** ties, months with no scheduled session in a category, and detailed grade aggregation for the monthly score.

## 12. Private coaching: quote each date, verify before teaching

Anyone can enquire as a guest for free. A cohort student may request extra support in their current skill or private instruction in another without taking a second free seat. The private timetable, work, assessment and payment ledger are separate from the cohort record.

1. **Enquiry → match:** the central coordinator receives every request and matches a coach. Learner and coach agree dates, durations and learning needs by chat or phone. A request is not a paid or confirmed class.
2. **Written agreement → private profile invitation → payment:** coordinator records the itemised dates, durations and prices; learner and coach both receive the schedule. Prepay by day, week or month. Calculate each session/day separately, then add the lines; do not pool leftover minutes across dates.
