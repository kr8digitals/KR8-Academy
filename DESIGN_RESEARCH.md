# KR8 Academy — product & experience benchmark

Research pass completed for the KR8 Academy redesign. The goal was to identify reusable interaction principles—not copy another brand's layout or voice. KR8 Academy's distinct brief is a tuition-free, practitioner-led African creative school with both digital-skills and exam-preparation journeys.

## What KR8 should take—and what it should not

1. **One clear first decision.** Help visitors choose Digital Skills or Academics before asking them for a long profile.
2. **Outcome before feature list.** State what a learner will make, pass, or be able to do; show the schedule and requirements nearby.
3. **Proof with provenance.** Prefer real KR8 learner voices, instructor profiles, real curricula and verifiable credentials over invented statistics or generic stock imagery.
4. **Show the journey.** Make application, onboarding, weekly work, feedback and graduation feel like one connected path.
5. **Respect device and data constraints.** Mobile-first layouts, readable tap targets, compressed imagery and no forced autoplay.
6. **Progressive disclosure.** Keep the public site inviting; reserve longer personal, educational and consent fields for a guided application with a visible step indicator.
7. **Make accessibility part of the visual system.** Strong contrast, semantic landmarks, keyboard states, field-level errors, reduced-motion support and captions.
8. **Keep KR8's identity.** Retain the deep aubergine, magenta and warm paper palette, real studio photography, direct Nigerian context and the dual promise of creative opportunity plus academic support.

This benchmark informed the current project changes: a clearer no-provider application entry, a direct application option from account creation, a registration desk that reflects the active application workflow, and a reduced-bandwidth testimonial video treatment.
